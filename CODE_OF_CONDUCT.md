# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the Aether community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully
- Focusing on the long-term health of the project and community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language
- Personal attacks or bad-faith engagement
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct.

## Scope
This policy applies within project spaces and in public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting
Report incidents to the maintainers via the security/support channels in `SECURITY.md` and `SUPPORT.md`.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may take any action deemed appropriate, including temporary or permanent bans.
